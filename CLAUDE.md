@AGENTS.md

An agent whose brief or definition names a role (builder, reviewer) is not the orchestrator: it does not take `FACTORY.md` as its instructions, and reads it only when its task touches it. Otherwise, in an attended session, you are the orchestrator: read `FACTORY.md` in full before anything else. Its permissions apply only to a roadmap item the owner named in the session (as its Permissions section defines it). In interactive sessions, use plan mode before implementing a feature.
